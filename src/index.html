Stamp Maker — professional rubber stamps for Microsoft Word

Design and insert APPROVED, CONFIDENTIAL, RECEIVED, PAID, official seals, date stamps, signature stamps and custom text — as real stamps that sit on top of your document without moving a single line of text.

What it does

  • 15 ready-made designs: APPROVED, REJECTED, CONFIDENTIAL, RECEIVED, PAID, COPY, ORIGINAL, DRAFT, URGENT, VERIFIED, DATE, SIGNATURE, official seal, diagonal, custom
  • Custom stamps with two text lines — perfect for office/station stamps (e.g. officer-in-charge stamps)
  • Change text, font, size, bold/italic/underline, alignment, ink color, border style & thickness, opacity, rotation
  • Live preview before you insert
  • Stamps are floating shapes wrapped in front of text — drag, resize and rotate them freely
  • They never push paragraphs, add blank lines, or reflow your document
  • Stamps stay put when saving, reopening, printing, and exporting to PDF
  • Edit, duplicate or delete any stamp you've already inserted

Install in 3 steps

  1. Download the manifest file — stamp-maker-manifest.xml (right-click → Save link as…).
  2. In Word (Windows), go to Insert → Add-ins → My Add-ins → Upload My Add-in.
  3. Select the downloaded file and click Open. A Stamp Maker group appears on the Home tab — click Design Stamp.
Requirements: Microsoft Word desktop (Microsoft 365) on Windows or Mac. Not available in Word for the web, Word 2013/2016/2019, or iPad — the add-in shows a clear message there. On Mac, there is no "Upload My Add-in" button: copy the manifest into ~/Library/Containers/com.microsoft.Word/Data/Documents/wef, then restart Word.

For organizations

Administrators can deploy Stamp Maker to every user at once via the Microsoft 365 admin center → Settings → Integrated apps (Centralized Deployment). Just upload the same manifest file and assign it to your users.
