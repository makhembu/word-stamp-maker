Get started

Installed in under a minute

  1. Download the installer: stamp-maker-setup.zip — a small zip with everything you need.
  2. Unzip it and double-click Install Stamp Maker (install-windows.bat on Windows, install-mac.command on Mac), then press Enter. That's the whole install — no files to move, no XML to touch.
  3. Open Word and you'll see a Stamp Maker group on the Home tab: click Design Stamp and start stamping.
Prefer to install by hand? Windows: Word → Insert → Add-ins → My Add-ins → Upload My Add-in → choose manifest.xml. Mac: copy that file into ~/Library/Containers/com.microsoft.Word/Data/Documents/wef. Requirements: Word desktop (Microsoft 365) on Windows or Mac — not Word for the web, Word 2013/2016/2019, or iPad.
FAQ

Good questions, straight answers

What exactly is Stamp Maker?

It is a Microsoft Word add-in — a small program that runs inside Word, not a separate website or app. Installing it adds a Design Stamp button to Word's Home tab. Click it and a task pane opens where you pick a design, type your text, and press the stamp onto the page.

Which versions of Word does it work with?

Word desktop with a Microsoft 365 subscription, on Windows or Mac. It does not run in Word for the web, on iPad, or in the older one-time-purchase versions (Word 2013, 2016, 2019). If Word can't support it, the add-in detects that and shows a clear message instead of failing silently.

Does it work on Mac?

Yes. The download zip includes install-mac.command for macOS — double-click it (right-click → Open the first time, so macOS lets it run). The add-in then appears under Home → Add-ins in Word for Mac, with the same features as Windows.

Is it really free? Do you collect my data?

Free, with no account, no ads, and no tracking. Stamps are designed and rendered on your own computer and inserted into your document as ordinary Word pictures — your document never leaves your machine. The add-in itself is loaded from the web the way normal Word add-ins are, so Word needs internet to fetch it when it opens; after that, everything runs locally and the stamps are saved inside your .docx file.

Will inserting a stamp mess up my document?

No. Stamps are floating images wrapped in front of text, so they sit on top of your page like a physical rubber stamp: no paragraphs are added, no blank lines appear, and nothing gets pushed to another page. The add-in ships with an automated test suite that verifies exactly this on every build.

Can I edit a stamp after it's inserted?

Yes. The Stamps tab lists every stamp the add-in created in the open document: edit the text or design in place (position and rotation are preserved), duplicate it, or delete it. You can also drag, resize, and rotate any stamp directly in Word.

How do I uninstall it?

Windows: double-click uninstall-windows.bat (included in the download zip). Mac: delete the file ~/Library/Containers/com.microsoft.Word/Data/Documents/wef/stamp-maker.xml, then restart Word.
